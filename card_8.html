Step 3 — Develop the Final Plan or Draft

Use this when

Your plan seems logical but you want a careful review before investing more time in the final product.

Prompt

Here is my current plan, thesis, argument, method, structure, or approach: [paste or describe it] The assignment requires: [list requirements] Please act like a careful reviewer. Look for: - weak assumptions; - missing steps; - unsupported claims; - gaps in reasoning; - duplicated effort; - unclear organization; - unrealistic expectations; - missing evidence; - contradictions; - places where the project may fail to meet the rubric. Identify the most important issues in priority order. For each issue: - explain why it matters; - identify what I should investigate, clarify, or revise; - suggest a question I should answer before moving on. Do not complete the project for me.

Before you act: Use criticism as a tool. Finding weaknesses before the final draft is easier than discovering them at submission time.
