Step 7 — Submit the Project

Step 7 — Submit the Project

Use this when

AI helped during the project and you want to ensure the final submission truthfully represents your own thinking, evidence, work, and voice.

Prompt

I am preparing the final submission for this academic project: [describe project] Here is the assignment or rubric: [paste or summarize] Here is the part of my final submission I want to review: [paste text or describe material] Here is what I actually did and understand: [describe your real work, decisions, evidence, and understanding] Please review the submission for authenticity and accuracy. Check for: 1. statements that sound more advanced than my demonstrated understanding; 2. claims about research, evidence, data, work, or decisions I did not actually complete; 3. technical or academic words I may not be able to explain; 4. conclusions stronger than my evidence supports; 5. generic AI-style wording that does not sound like my own explanation; 6. missing references to important work, revisions, or difficulties that actually occurred; 7. places where I should replace generic language with specific evidence from my project. For every concern, explain what I should verify or rewrite. Do not rewrite the whole submission for me. Finish with a checklist titled: READY TO SUBMIT WHEN...

Before you submit: Your final submission should represent your own understanding, choices, evidence, and effort. AI can support the process, but it should not create a false record of work or understanding.
