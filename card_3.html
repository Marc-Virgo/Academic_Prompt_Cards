Card 3 — “I need to make my project idea simpler.”

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Use this when

Your idea is too broad, ambitious, complicated, or time-consuming.

Prompt

Here is my project idea: [describe idea] The assignment requirements are: [list requirements] My deadline is: [deadline] My concern is: [describe why the project may be too large or complicated] Please help me reduce the scope while keeping the main purpose. Give me three versions: - basic; - moderate; - ambitious. For each version, explain: - what I would complete; - what I would leave out; - how well it still meets the assignment requirements; - what risks or difficulties remain.

Before you act: Choose a scope you can complete well. A focused, complete project is usually stronger than an unfinished ambitious one.
