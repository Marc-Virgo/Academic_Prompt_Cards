Card 0A — “I finished a step. What do I do next?”

Throughout the Project

Throughout the Project

Use this when

You completed part of an academic project and are unsure what the next logical step should be.

Prompt

I am working on this academic project: [describe the assignment or project]. So far, I have completed these steps: [list what you have done]. My final goal is: [describe the finished product, presentation, report, investigation, performance, or other required outcome]. Please summarize where I am in the process, identify what still needs to be done, and suggest the next 3 practical steps I should take. Explain why each step comes next and make sure the suggestions fit the assignment requirements, time available, and resources I can use.
Before you act: Compare the suggested next steps with the original assignment, rubric, teacher instructions, deadline, and available resources.
