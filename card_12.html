Card 12 — “A part of my project is not working as expected.”

Step 4 — Create the Work

Step 4 — Create the Work

Use this when

A model, experiment, presentation element, calculation, research method, data collection step, or other project component is not producing the expected result.

Prompt

This part of my project is: [describe it] It should do or show this: [expected result] Instead, this is happening: [actual result] Here is what I have already tried: [list attempts] Please give me a troubleshooting sequence starting with the simplest and most likely causes. For each step: 1. tell me what to check; 2. explain what the check will tell me; 3. describe the expected result; 4. suggest what to investigate next depending on the result. Do not assume the first explanation is correct. Help me test possibilities one at a time.
Before you act: Change one thing at a time so you can tell what actually caused the problem or improvement.
