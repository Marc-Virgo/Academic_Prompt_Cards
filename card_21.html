Step 6 — Finalize & Document

Use this when

You need to present, demonstrate, discuss, defend, or summarize the completed project.

Prompt

I need to explain my academic project to someone else. My project is: [describe project] The main question or goal was: [goal] My main result or conclusion is: [result] The strongest evidence is: [evidence] The hardest part was: [challenge] The most important change I made was: [revision] I learned: [learning] Please help me create a clear short explanation suitable for a Grade 9–12 class presentation. Include: - the purpose; - the process; - the strongest evidence; - one important challenge or revision; - the conclusion; - what I learned. Keep the language natural and appropriate for a student speaking aloud.

Before you present: Practise saying it aloud. Make sure you can answer follow-up questions without relying on the AI-generated wording.
