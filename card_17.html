Card 17 — “I need to recover from a major problem or failure.”

Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Use this when

A major part of the project did not work, evidence was weak, a draft failed to meet expectations, or your original approach is no longer viable.

Prompt

A major part of my project has failed or is not working. The goal was: [describe intended result] What happened was: [describe problem] The evidence I have is: [feedback, results, data, comments, observations, etc.] I think the possible cause might be: [your hypothesis] Please help me analyze the problem. Separate the response into: WHAT WE KNOW Facts supported by the evidence. WHAT WE THINK Possible explanations that are still hypotheses. WHAT WE NEED TO FIND OUT The most useful missing information. POSSIBLE RECOVERY OPTIONS Several realistic ways forward. For each option explain: - what would need to change; - how much existing work could be kept; - likely time required; - what should be checked afterward; - any new weaknesses the change could introduce. Recommend the best next investigation rather than simply replacing my project with a new one.
Before you act: A failed approach is evidence. Understand what went wrong before deciding whether to revise, narrow, replace, or restart.
