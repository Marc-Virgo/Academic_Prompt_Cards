Step 3 — Develop the Final Plan or Draft

Use this when

You have a substantial draft, outline, research plan, lab plan, presentation plan, or other near-final structure and want to know whether it is ready to execute or revise.

Prompt

Here is my current draft or project plan: [paste the text or attach images/pages] The assignment criteria are: [paste or summarize them] Please review my work as if you were checking whether it is ready for the next stage. Evaluate: 1. Completeness 2. Organization 3. Clarity 4. Use of evidence or supporting information 5. Logic or reasoning 6. Alignment with assignment criteria 7. Feasibility for the remaining time For each important assignment criterion, classify it as: - Clearly addressed - Partially addressed - Not addressed - Cannot be determined Give an overall readiness score out of 100: - Completeness: /20 - Organization: /15 - Clarity: /15 - Evidence/support: /20 - Reasoning: /15 - Alignment with criteria: /15 Then provide: - three strongest aspects; - three highest-priority revisions; - anything important that is missing; - an overall recommendation: READY TO CONTINUE READY WITH MINOR REVISIONS REVISE BEFORE CONTINUING MAJOR INFORMATION MISSING Do not rewrite the entire project for me. Tell me what I should improve myself.

Before you act: AI feedback is advisory. Check every recommendation against the actual rubric, assignment sheet, and teacher feedback.
