Step 2 — Develop an Initial Approach

Step 2 — Develop an Initial Approach

Use this when

You have a first concept, outline, thesis, research question, plan, draft structure, or other preliminary approach.

Prompt

Here is my current project idea or early plan: [paste or describe it] The purpose of the project is: [explain purpose] The assignment criteria are: [list criteria] The constraints are: [time, length, format, sources, required methods, etc.] Please critique my initial approach. Evaluate: - clarity of purpose; - focus; - feasibility; - organization; - evidence I will need; - likely weaknesses; - unnecessary complexity; - whether it addresses the assignment criteria. Then suggest the most important improvements I should consider before developing the full project.
Before you act: Decide which suggestions actually improve your project. You do not need to use every suggestion.
