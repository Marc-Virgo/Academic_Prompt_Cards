Card 13 — “I need to learn how to complete an unfamiliar academic process.”

Step 4 — Create the Work

Step 4 — Create the Work

Use this when

You need to perform a research, laboratory, writing, analysis, presentation, citation, data, or other academic process you have not done before.

Prompt

I need to complete this academic process: [describe process] It is for this project: [describe project] My teacher has given these instructions: [paste or summarize instructions] My experience with this process is: [none / beginner / some experience] Please explain: 1. what the process is for; 2. the normal workflow; 3. what I should prepare before starting; 4. common beginner mistakes; 5. what evidence or output I should have when finished; 6. what questions I should ask my teacher if anything is unclear. Do not override teacher instructions or assignment-specific rules.
Before you act: Teacher instructions, course procedures, safety requirements, academic-integrity rules, and school policies take priority over AI advice.
