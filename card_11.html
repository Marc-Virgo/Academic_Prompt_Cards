Card 11 — “My code, formula, spreadsheet, or digital work does not work.”

Step 4 — Create the Work

Step 4 — Create the Work

Use this when

A digital or computational part of your academic project has an error or unexpected result.

Prompt

Here is the code, formula, calculation, spreadsheet logic, or digital process: [paste it] It is supposed to do this: [expected result] Instead, it does this: [actual result or error] Please: 1. identify the likely problem; 2. explain it in student-friendly language; 3. suggest the smallest correction that may fix it; 4. explain what changed and why; 5. suggest one test I should perform afterward. Do not change unrelated parts unless necessary.
Before you act: Do not blindly paste a correction. Read the explanation and make sure you understand why it works.
