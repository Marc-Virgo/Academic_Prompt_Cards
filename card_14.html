Card 14 — “I am having difficulty completing this part.”

Step 4 — Create the Work

Step 4 — Create the Work

Use this when

You understand the goal but are struggling with a specific writing, research, analysis, calculation, organization, creation, or presentation task.

Prompt

I am trying to complete this part of my project: [describe task] The difficulty I am having is: [describe problem] I expected: [expected result] Instead: [actual result] I have already tried: [list attempts] The assignment requirements are: [list relevant requirements] Please help me troubleshoot the difficulty. First identify several possible causes. Then rank them from: MOST LIKELY AND EASIEST TO CHECK to LESS LIKELY OR MORE DIFFICULT TO INVESTIGATE. For each cause: 1. explain what may be happening; 2. suggest a simple way I can check it; 3. suggest a possible adjustment; 4. identify anything I should confirm with my teacher. Prefer small corrections before recommending that I start over.

Before you act: Do not let troubleshooting turn into having AI complete the assignment for you. Use the suggestions to guide your own next attempt.
