Step 2 — Develop an Initial Approach

Step 2 — Develop an Initial Approach

Use this when

You have created a mind map, handwritten outline, concept map, storyboard, planning sheet, poster sketch, diagram, or other visual project plan and want feedback before continuing.

Prompt

I have attached an image of my project plan. The assignment criteria are: [paste or describe the criteria] The purpose of my project is: [describe purpose] Please analyze the uploaded image as an early project plan. Evaluate: 1. Clarity — Is the main idea easy to identify? 2. Organization — Do the parts appear logically connected? 3. Simplicity — Is anything unnecessarily complicated or repetitive? 4. Feasibility — Does the plan appear realistic for a Grade 9–12 project? 5. Completeness — Are important requirements or sections missing? 6. Evidence — Does the plan show where research, examples, data, quotations, calculations, or other support may be needed? 7. Project criteria — Which criteria appear addressed, partially addressed, or missing? Give an overall score out of 100: - Clarity: /20 - Organization: /20 - Feasibility: /20 - Completeness: /20 - Alignment with criteria: /20 Then identify: - three strengths; - three important improvements; - the next three steps I should consider. Do not create the whole project for me. Help me improve my own plan.

Before you act: AI may misread handwriting, arrows, small text, or visual relationships. Verify its interpretation against your actual plan and assignment instructions.
