Step 3 — Develop the Final Plan or Draft

Step 3 — Develop the Final Plan or Draft

Use this when

You need to use unfamiliar software, a research database, spreadsheet, presentation tool, graphing tool, citation manager, document editor, or another academic tool.

Prompt

I need to learn how to use: [tool/software/database] For this purpose: [describe what you need to accomplish] My skill level is: [beginner/intermediate/advanced] Please give me a beginner-friendly quick-start guide focused only on what I need for this project. Include: 1. the basic workflow; 2. the most important features or commands; 3. common beginner mistakes; 4. how to save or export my work correctly; 5. a small practice task related to my project; 6. the next step I should try. If the tool has features that could accidentally alter, delete, publish, or share work, warn me before those steps.
Before you act: Follow teacher instructions, school technology rules, privacy expectations, and assignment requirements first.
