Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Use this when

A photograph, graph, chart, handwritten page, model, poster, slide, calculation, lab setup, or other visual evidence may help explain what went wrong.

Prompt

I have attached one or more images showing a problem with my project. The project is: [describe project] The expected result is: [describe expected result] The specific problem is: [describe problem] Please inspect the uploaded image carefully. 1. Describe only what you can actually observe. 2. Identify possible causes. 3. Separate visible evidence from inference. 4. Rank each possible cause as likely, possible, or uncertain. 5. Suggest simple checks or questions that could help determine the cause. 6. Suggest reasonable next steps. 7. Identify what cannot be determined from the image alone. 8. Tell me what additional image, data, measurement, source, or explanation would help. Do not claim certainty if the evidence is incomplete.
Before you act: Images can be misleading because of scale, cropping, handwriting, perspective, or missing context. Use the analysis to generate questions and checks, not as proof.
