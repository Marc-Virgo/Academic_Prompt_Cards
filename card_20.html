Step 6 — Finalize & Document

Use this when

You have notes, sources, drafts, feedback, data, revisions, or other evidence and need to organize them into a clear record of the project.

Prompt

I need to organize the documentation for my academic project. My project is: [describe project] Here is the evidence I collected: [paste or summarize notes, drafts, sources, feedback, data, decisions, revisions, etc.] Please organize this evidence chronologically and logically. Use sections such as: 1. Project Goal or Research Question 2. Initial Research 3. Planning and Early Ideas 4. Development of the Project 5. Evidence, Data, or Analysis 6. Problems and Revisions 7. Final Result 8. Evaluation 9. What I Learned 10. Sources / References Identify any important information that appears to be missing. Do not invent missing events, data, sources, decisions, or results.

Before you submit: Your documentation should describe what actually happened. Include important changes, failed approaches, and feedback rather than pretending the project developed perfectly.
