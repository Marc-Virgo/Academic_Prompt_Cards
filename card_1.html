Card 1 — “I do not understand what I am supposed to do.”

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Use this when

The assignment, project, rubric, or instructions feel confusing.

Prompt

Here is the assignment or task: [paste or describe the assignment] I do not fully understand what I am supposed to do. Please: 1. rewrite the task in simpler language; 2. identify the main goal; 3. list the required parts; 4. identify any important constraints; 5. explain what a successful finished result should include; 6. identify anything I should confirm with my teacher.
Before you act: Compare the explanation with the original assignment and rubric. If something is missing or uncertain, ask your teacher.
