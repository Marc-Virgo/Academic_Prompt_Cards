Step 1 — Research & Plan

Use this when

You have several possible topics, methods, formats, sources, or approaches and need to choose among them.

Prompt

I am deciding between these options: [list options] My project goal is: [goal] The assignment criteria are: [list criteria] My constraints are: [time, length, available sources, format, technology, group size, etc.] Please compare the options in a table. For each option, evaluate: - how well it fits the assignment; - difficulty; - time required; - likely quality of available evidence; - originality; - major risks or weaknesses. Then recommend the strongest option for my situation and explain why.

Before you act: Use the comparison to support your own decision. Check course requirements and teacher expectations before committing.
