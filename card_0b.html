Card B — “What should I document right now?”

Throughout the Project

Throughout the Project

Use this when

You have completed some project work and want to record evidence you may need later for a final report, reflection, presentation, bibliography, process log, or portfolio.

Prompt

I am currently at this stage of my academic project: [research / planning / drafting / creating / analyzing / revising / presenting / other] My project is: [describe project] Today I did the following: [describe what you worked on] Please tell me what evidence and information I should record now so I can accurately explain my process later. Consider whether I should keep: - research notes; - sources and citations; - questions I investigated; - ideas I considered; - decisions I made and why; - drafts or outlines; - calculations or data; - images, screenshots, or files; - feedback I received; - changes I made; - problems I encountered; - unsuccessful approaches; - successful strategies; - AI assistance I used; - what I learned; - what I plan to do next. Separate your answer into: RECORD NOW — information that could easily be forgotten. SAVE — files, drafts, sources, images, data, or evidence I should keep. OPTIONAL — useful information that may improve my final reflection or report. Then give me a short project-log template I can complete for today. Do not invent anything I did.
Before you act: Document what actually happened, including changes and unsuccessful attempts. Accurate process evidence is more useful than a perfect-looking reconstruction later.
