Step 7 — Submit the Project

Step 7 — Submit the Project

Use this when

You have finished the project and want to check whether you personally understand the work well enough to submit or defend it.

Prompt

I am about to submit this academic project: [describe project] The project requirements are: [paste or summarize requirements] Here is a summary of what I did, found, created, analyzed, changed, and learned: [paste summary] Please quiz me to check whether I actually understand my project. Ask questions about: 1. the purpose or research question; 2. important decisions I made; 3. key subject concepts; 4. evidence, sources, data, or examples; 5. methods I used; 6. problems I encountered; 7. revisions I made; 8. my conclusion or final result; 9. limitations; 10. what I learned. Ask one question at a time. Do not give me the answer before I respond. After each answer: - tell me whether it shows good understanding; - identify anything important I missed; - ask a follow-up if necessary. At the end summarize: UNDERSTAND WELL REVIEW BEFORE SUBMITTING DO NOT YET UNDERSTAND Then tell me what I should review.
Before you submit: If you cannot explain the central ideas, evidence, decisions, and conclusions in your own words, you are not finished yet.
