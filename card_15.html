Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Use this when

You have a draft, product, argument, solution, analysis, presentation, experiment, or other project result and need a systematic way to check whether it meets the assignment.

Prompt

I have completed this: [describe project or current version] It is supposed to meet these requirements: [list criteria] Please create a review or test plan. For each check, identify: 1. what requirement or feature is being checked; 2. what I should do; 3. what evidence I should look for; 4. what would count as success; 5. what would count as a problem; 6. what that problem might suggest; 7. what I should record. Start with simple checks before more detailed ones.
Before you act: Record the results of your review. A vague feeling that something is 'good enough' is less useful than evidence tied to the rubric.
