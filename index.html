AI prompt cards for academic projects in Grades 9–12 — any subject area.

Academic Project Prompt Guide

Select the stage that best matches where you are in your project, then choose the question that describes the help you need.

The Autodidact Rule: What am I trying to do? What have I already tried? What do I need to learn next? What tool can help me learn it? How will I verify the answer? What action will I take now?

Throughout the Project

Use these cards at any point to decide what comes next or preserve evidence of your process.

Card 0A — “I finished a step. What do I do next?”Card 0B — “What should I document right now?”

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Understand the assignment, choose a direction, control scope, compare approaches, and organize the work.

Card 1 — “I do not understand what I am supposed to do.”Card 2 — “I need a project plan.”Card 3 — “I need to make my project idea simpler.”Card 4 — “I need to compare possible approaches.”Card 5 — “I’m not feeling particularly creative. I need help coming up with project ideas.”

Step 2 — Develop an Initial Approach

Create and improve an early idea, outline, concept map, storyboard, or visual plan.

Card 6 — “I need to improve my initial idea.”Card 7 — “I need AI to evaluate my visual plan.”

Step 3 — Develop the Final Plan or Draft

Review the logic, learn needed academic tools, and assess whether the plan or draft is ready.

Card 8 — “I need to debug my thinking.”Card 9 — “I need to learn a new academic tool.”Card 10 — “I need AI to assess my draft or plan.”

Step 4 — Create the Work

Complete the research, writing, analysis, calculations, presentation, experiment, model, or other project components.

Card 11 — “My code, formula, spreadsheet, or digital work does not work.”Card 12 — “A part of my project is not working as expected.”Card 13 — “I need to learn how to complete an unfamiliar academic process.”Card 14 — “I am having difficulty completing this part.”

Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Check the work against the criteria, diagnose problems, learn from failure, and improve the project.

Card 15 — “I need a test or review plan.”Card 16 — “I am stuck.”Card 17 — “I need to recover from a major problem or failure.”Card 18 — “I need AI to inspect a visual problem.”

Step 6 — Finalize & Document

Understand the result, organize the project record, and prepare to explain the completed work.

Card 19 — “I need to understand why my result makes sense.”Card 20 — “I need to organize my project documentation.”Card 21 — “I need to explain or present my project.”

Step 7 — Submit the Project

Verify understanding and make sure the final submission honestly represents the student’s own work.

Card 22 — “I need to make sure I understand before submitting.”Card 23 — “I need to make sure my final work is actually mine.”

Remember

AI can help you interpret instructions, plan, research, troubleshoot, revise, and reflect. You remain responsible for checking sources, following teacher instructions, meeting academic-integrity expectations, and understanding the work you submit.
