Card 2 — “I need a project plan.”

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Use this when

You understand the assignment but need help organizing the work.

Prompt

I need to complete this academic project: [describe project] The requirements are: [list requirements] My deadline is: [deadline] The resources I can use are: [list resources] My current progress is: [describe progress] Please break the project into logical phases and tasks. For each phase: - explain what I should accomplish; - identify what evidence or product I should have when the phase is complete; - point out likely difficulties; - suggest a realistic checkpoint. Then recommend the best first step.

Before you act: Make sure the plan fits the actual deadline, course expectations, and resources you have.
