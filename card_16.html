Step 5 — Review, Test & Revise

Use this when

You have reviewed or revised the work and still do not know what to try next.

Prompt

I am stuck on this part of my project: [describe problem] The assignment expects: [expected outcome] My current work does this: [actual outcome] Here are the checks, feedback, or revisions I have already tried: [list them] Please help me troubleshoot systematically. First summarize what the evidence currently tells us. Then: 1. identify the most likely causes; 2. rank them from easiest and most useful to investigate first; 3. suggest one check or revision for each cause; 4. explain what each result would mean; 5. recommend the most reasonable next step. If there is not enough information, tell me what additional evidence would be most useful.
Before you act: Revise one important thing at a time when possible. That makes it easier to see whether the change actually improved the work.
