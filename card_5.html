Card 5 — “I’m not feeling particularly creative. I need help coming up with project ideas.”

Step 1 — Research & Plan

Use this when

You understand the assignment but are having trouble finding an interesting topic, question, or project direction.

Prompt

Here is the assignment: [paste or describe it] The requirements are: [list important requirements] The subject area is: [subject/course] My interests include: [list interests, hobbies, issues, topics, or questions] My available resources are: [list important limits or resources] Please generate 10 different project ideas that fit the assignment. For each idea, briefly explain: - the central question or goal; - what I would produce; - what research or evidence I might need; - how difficult it would be; - what could make it interesting or original. Then recommend the 3 ideas that seem most realistic and worthwhile.
Before you act: Do not simply choose the AI’s favourite idea. Choose a topic you genuinely want to investigate and can complete within the assignment constraints.
