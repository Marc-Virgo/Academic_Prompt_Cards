Step 6 — Finalize & Document

Step 6 — Finalize & Document

Use this when

You have a result, conclusion, solution, interpretation, or finished product but want to understand the underlying idea well enough to explain it.

Prompt

I completed this: [describe result or project] It works, makes sense, or produced this conclusion: [describe outcome] But I do not fully understand why. Please explain the underlying concept step by step using: - simple language; - the important subject-specific idea; - one analogy if useful; - one example connected to my project. Then ask me to explain the concept back in my own words.
Before you act: If you cannot explain the important ideas in your own words, keep reviewing before you submit or present.
